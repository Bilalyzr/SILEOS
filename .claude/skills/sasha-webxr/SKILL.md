Blueprint 13.4 + 8.6. Load for XR work.

- NO learning objective may ever require a headset. Headset = upgrade tier T0 only.
- Quest 3S browser (WebXR) is the v1 target; a native app is optional later.
- Under-13: session time limits with mandatory break prompts.
- Motion sickness: teleport/turn default, no forced camera motion, vignette on movement, IPD/height calibration prompts, physical-space warning before room-scale.
- Photosensitive epilepsy: flash-rate analysis on animated content in the pipeline.
- Explicit consent for camera/room-scan; clear permission indicators.
