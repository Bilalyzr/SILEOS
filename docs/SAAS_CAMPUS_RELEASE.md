# SashaInfinity SaaS and Sunrise UI release

Built for schools and colleges, 10 September 2026.

## Product changes

- A light-orange gradient and warm glass surfaces across the shared application theme. Dashboard banners, page headers, navigation, common buttons, dialogs and forms follow the same palette. Success, danger and learning visualization colors retain their meaning. Reduced-motion and high-contrast preferences are preserved.
- A reusable brand banner on instructor/student dashboards and campus pages. Campus managers can save their own headline, supporting text and uploaded artwork in Settings. Uploaded campus artwork is authenticated, image-validated, resized and re-encoded; it is not exposed as a public static upload.
- The shared Share dialog previews matching artwork, accepts an optional local image, downloads a 1200 × 630 PNG, supports native file sharing where available, and offers link/caption copying and social composers. Local custom images are not automatically uploaded to a social network. Public courses have a server-rendered share page and PNG for crawlers; drafts are excluded. Certificates keep their existing verification share URLs. English and Tamil banner assets have been visually checked.
- CSV onboarding previews up to 500 rows before creating invitations atomically. Invalid rows or insufficient capacity prevent the whole import. Duplicate accounts and existing invitations are skipped. No passwords are created or distributed by import. Recipients join through verified accounts.
- Optional institution invitation email delivery with durable jobs, queued/sent/failed states, retry limits, and a startup worker that resumes queued work. SMTP failures do not pretend to be delivery success. Sending is initiated by an authorized manager through the invitation action.
- Academic terms/semesters, daily batch attendance, classroom assessments, numeric grading and individual feedback. Students see their own academic rows; staff access is limited to their institution. Existing online quizzes and assignment submissions retain their course gradebook.
- A private institutional resource library, with institution-wide or batch-specific PDF/text/CSV resources. Reads and downloads check active membership and batch audience. Files live in authenticated storage rather than the legacy public upload directory. Resources in use by private lessons cannot be removed until detached.
- Private campus courses with drafts, lessons, protected resource attachments, publishing/unpublishing, learner completion and staff progress reporting. These use dedicated institution-scoped tables and APIs. Private learning does not create public LMS enrollments, appear in the shared catalog, or bypass a paid catalog course. Unpublishing retains progress while withdrawing student access.
- Guardian consent: knowing a student's email no longer grants access. A verified student approves, declines or revokes a guardian request from their dashboard. Legacy links without approval do not reveal digests; guardians must request approval again. Guardian digests are not automatically sent through WhatsApp; any future channel routing must use the account-wide consent, approved templates and audience checks described below.
- Razorpay institution subscriptions, provider-sourced price review, pinned plan identity, checkout recovery, renewal cancellation, and authenticated invoice lists. A mandate activation alone does not upgrade capacity: a verified captured subscription charge establishes the paid period. Duplicate/out-of-order events do not roll paid access backward or resurrect a terminal subscription. Capacity falls back to Starter when the paid period ends; existing data is retained.
- A guided campus launchpad derives its checklist from real profile, department, people, batch, timetable and announcement data. Managers may hide the guide without changing completion state.
- A shared timetable covers classes, examinations, meetings, holidays and events. Optional daily/weekly recurrence creates bounded occurrences, and the server blocks overlapping batch, teacher or room bookings.
- A private announcement inbox, learner goals and term report cards add the daily return loop. Report cards combine existing institution assessments, attendance and teacher feedback with configurable grade bands and a branded downloadable PDF.
- Meta WhatsApp Cloud API is an account-wide SashaInfinity communication channel. Every authenticated user manages one global phone and consent record through **Communication preferences** at `/communication-preferences`. Institution campaigns reuse it while still enforcing active campus membership, and the global **Communications Center** at `/admin/communications` gives platform administrators account-wide campaign controls with an explicit allowed role audience. One-time inbound JOIN challenges confirm ownership, approved-template allow-lists constrain content, optional HTTPS image headers support branded messages, and durable jobs plus signed webhooks record delivery/read/failure updates. Every send re-checks consent and its audience rule. `STOP` withdraws consent across SashaInfinity for every account matching that normalized phone. Campaign audiences expand atomically in bounded keyset batches; each local delivery tick fairly interleaves institution and platform queues with at most four workers. Delivery is at-least-once across an ambiguous provider timeout or worker crash; campaign creation and webhook processing remain idempotent, and operations should monitor rare duplicate delivery.

## Capacity and access boundaries

Starter permits 100 active members plus pending invitation reservations, 10 batches and 20 total private/connected courses. A verified owner may create up to five institutions. Current resource limits are 100 files per institution, 5 MB per file; image uploads are limited to 5 MB and 20 megapixels. There are up to 100 lessons per private course, 100 assessments per batch, and 100 academic terms per institution. These bounds are deliberate and should be measured against pilot needs before increasing them.

Institution membership roles are independent of global LMS roles. Being a platform administrator does not grant automatic access to campus academic data or private files. Platform administrators have narrowly defined subscription recovery and plan-request administration endpoints. Course authors and institution managers can edit private course lessons; students outside a course's batch cannot access it. Learning completion is a learner action, not an automatic claim of mastery or an issued certificate.

The existing public/shared LMS catalog remains a shared catalog. Connected legacy courses keep their original visibility, editor permissions and purchase rules. Confidential material should be authored in the new private campus course area. This release does not infer ownership from old free-text institution labels or automatically migrate legacy course/media records into tenants. Existing public/static LMS uploads must not be treated as private campus storage.

## Production setup

1. Back up the deployed database and prove a restore in staging. Run `alembic upgrade head` in the configured backend environment. This release adds `0031` (academic operations, branding, consent, subscriptions), `0032` (private learning), `0033` (daily campus operations and initial WhatsApp delivery) and `0034` (account-wide WhatsApp contacts and campaigns) after `0030`. Migration `0034` maps legacy campus contact consent to its owning user account; the legacy table is retained for rollback/archive compatibility and is no longer the active consent source. Deploy the matching frontend and backend together. Apply migrations before enabling the new routes.
2. Configure `FRONTEND_URL` with the real HTTPS frontend origin. It is also used by invitation links and public course share metadata. Verify reverse-proxy routes for `/api/v1/share/*` and `/api/v1/institutions/*` and serve the bundled `/brand/` and font assets.
3. Configure the existing SMTP host, port, user, password and sender settings. Test invitation success/failure, account verification and password reset against a staging mailbox. The campus worker retries queued work on startup; failed sends are retained for manager retry, up to five attempts. SMTP is not an exactly-once transport: a connection failure after delivery can result in a duplicate invitation email on retry.
4. Create agreed institutional plans in Razorpay, then set `CAMPUS_CAMPUS_PLAN_ID` and/or `CAMPUS_ENTERPRISE_PLAN_ID`. Use the existing Razorpay key/secret settings and `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET`. Do not put credentials in the frontend. Checkout uses quantity one and 12 provider billing cycles, with the actual interval/period and price shown before authorization. Plans are not invented by the application.
5. Enable and test the existing signature-verified webhook inbox with `subscription.charged`, `subscription.activated`, `subscription.pending`, `subscription.halted`, `subscription.cancelled` and `subscription.completed`. Verify duplicate delivery, reordered events, expiration, failures and period-end cancellation using provider test mode. Confirm the invoice links and accounting/refund procedures with the merchant configuration. A timed-out creation keeps a durable attempt and blocks duplicate checkout; platform administrators can reconcile the matching provider subscription from the institution hub.
6. Run `scripts/campus_release_check.py` with the deployed backend virtual environment. It checks prerequisites without printing secrets or mutating data, and now requires migration head exactly `0034` plus the global WhatsApp tables. It is not a replacement for a security review, recovery drill or load test.
7. Follow [the WhatsApp Cloud API setup guide](WHATSAPP_CLOUD_SETUP.md). Configure Meta credentials in backend secrets, approve the exact templates, register `/api/v1/whatsapp/webhook`, retain the supplied 1 MB edge body limit, exercise global account opt-in, JOIN/STOP and delivery statuses in test mode, then enable institution and global Communications Center campaigns in controlled stages. Provider credentials must never be placed in frontend configuration.

Local preview: `backend/.venv/Scripts/python scripts/preview_campus.py` from the repository root, plus `npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1` from `frontend`. Data and generated credentials are under ignored `.local/`. The preview forces SMTP, institutional payment and WhatsApp provider configuration off. It binds to loopback and uses a synthetic SQLite database; it cannot send an external WhatsApp message and is not a production deployment.

## Verification

Automated checks cover cross-institution and batch access, suspension, guardian approval/revocation, CSV atomicity, invalid scores/dates/files, private course publishing and completion, blocked public leakage, billing price identity, uncertain-checkout recovery, captured-charge entitlements, duplicate emails, calendar conflicts, notification scope, learner goals, report cards, WhatsApp consent/signatures/idempotency and migration upgrade/downgrade paths. Account-wide WhatsApp acceptance must include a user without an institution, reuse across memberships, global opt-out/STOP, platform administrator authorization and delivery-time audience re-checks. Browser verification uses fictional Greenwood College data.

Current command outcomes and outstanding checks are recorded in `docs/INSTITUTION_BUILD.md`. PostgreSQL concurrency and migration execution, real gateway/email delivery, restoration of production backups, enterprise identity integration and realistic load are deployment acceptance work. No production account was charged, no real invitation email was sent, and no production database was migrated during this build.
