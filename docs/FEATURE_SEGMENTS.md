# Feature segments

Learning Labs is available in the student Learning segment and public navigation. Lab Studio belongs in instructor Content Studio and admin Learning & Content. See [Learning labs and redesign](LABS_AND_REDESIGN.md) for authoring, imports, notebooks and spatial-mode capabilities.

Navigation groups follow the job people are doing. Active groups open automatically; selecting a group in the compact rail expands it. Existing destinations and review counts are preserved.

| Role | Segments |
|---|---|
| Instructor | Teaching; Content Studio; Assessment & Support; Learners & Insights |
| Student | Learning; Progress; Explore; Career & Community |
| Admin | Learning & Content; People & Access; Commerce; Internships; Insights & Community |

Dashboard and account/settings remain directly accessible. Parent, SPOC, company and supervisory navigation retain their smaller role-specific menus. Recording lessons belong in the instructor Content Studio; approved lessons feed the existing curriculum and learning planner.

Course Upload & Backup belongs in Content Studio (instructor) and Learning & Content (admin). The administrator Operations Center groups Daily operations, People & courses, Learning outcomes, Revenue and System health within one page. Mobile Dashboard exposes a Learning workspace for plans and instructor intervention reviews; ended classes link to recording notes and transcripts.
