Think about it

Watch the pH curve build: it crawls, then leaps near the equivalence point where moles of OH⁻ = moles of H⁺. Phenolphthalein flashes pink just after it. Use 0.1 mL adds near the jump — real titrations are won by drop-by-drop care!
