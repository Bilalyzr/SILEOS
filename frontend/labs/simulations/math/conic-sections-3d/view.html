How to play

Drag to orbit the double cone. Slide the plane's tilt: at 0° the slice is a circle; tilt more for an ellipse; when the plane turns exactly parallel to the cone's side, the curve opens into a parabola; steeper still, the plane cuts both cones giving a hyperbola. The Greeks discovered all four shapes in this one object!
