Think about it

Sine is just the shadow of a rotating point — its height on a circle of radius 1. Cosine is the sideways shadow. Keep spinning and the values repeat every 2π: that's periodicity, the idea behind sound waves, AC electricity and tides. Drag the point to explore; watch sin and cos trade places every quarter turn (π/2).
