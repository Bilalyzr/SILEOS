Think about it

Slide θ: sin climbs to 1 at 90° while cos falls to 0 — they swap! tan θ = sin θ/cos θ explodes near 90°. The ratios depend only on the angle, never on the triangle's size — that's why trigonometry works for the Sun or a flagpole alike.
