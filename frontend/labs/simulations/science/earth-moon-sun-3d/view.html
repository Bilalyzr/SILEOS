Think about it

The Moon makes no light of its own — we only see the sunlit half. Slide the moon around Earth: the lit fraction we see waxes from new 🌑 to full 🌕 and back over 29.5 days. Now check eclipse alignment: shadow lands on the Moon (lunar eclipse) or on Earth (solar eclipse). Why don't eclipses happen every month?
