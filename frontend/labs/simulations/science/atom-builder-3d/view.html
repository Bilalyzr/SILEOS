Think about it

Red protons + grey neutrons huddle in the tiny nucleus — it holds 99.9% of the mass! Blue electrons race along shells K, L, M, N. Only the outermost shell decides chemistry: Sodium (2,8,1) is desperate to lose one electron — that's why it reacts so violently. Add a neutron: same element, new isotope!
