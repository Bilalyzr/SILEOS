Think about it

Each nucleus decays randomly, but a sample of billions follows a strict law: every half-life, exactly half the remaining atoms decay. After 2 half-lives: ¼ left. After 3: ⅛. That clock never resets — it's how carbon dating finds the age of mummies and fossils!
