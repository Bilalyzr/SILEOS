Think about it

Light arrives as photons of energy hν — one photon, one electron. If hν < φ (work function), no electrons ever — no matter how bright! Try red on zinc: nothing. Crank intensity: still nothing! But blue on cesium fires instantly. This is the evidence that won Einstein his Nobel.
