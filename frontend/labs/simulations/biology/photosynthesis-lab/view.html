Limiting factors

The rate is decided by the scarcest ingredient: no light means no photosynthesis at all (only respiration — watch the count go negative!). Push light to 100% and the CO₂ becomes the bottleneck. Too hot (above ~35°C) and the enzymes start to fail. This is the law of limiting factors.
