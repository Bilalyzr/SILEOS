Think about it

Three temperatures, repeat forever: 95°C unzips DNA, 55°C lets primers stick, 72°C lets polymerase copy. Each cycle doubles the DNA: 30 cycles = 2³⁰ ≈ a billion copies from one strand! That exponential amplification is why forensics can profile a single hair.
