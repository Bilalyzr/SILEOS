Experiments to try

Boyle: keep T fixed, halve the volume — pressure doubles.
Charles: keep V fixed, double T (K!) — pressure doubles.
Watch the particles: hotter = faster, smaller box = more hits per second on the walls. That's all pressure is!

Think about it

Temperature is the average kinetic energy of particles — at 100 K they crawl, at 600 K they swarm. Why does a pressure cooker cook faster? Why do tyres get firmer after a long drive?
