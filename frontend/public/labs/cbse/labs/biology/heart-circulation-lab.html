Think about it

Trace the journey: body → right atrium → right ventricle → lungs (pick up O₂) → left atrium → left ventricle → body. Blood passes the heart twice per loop — double circulation! Why is the left ventricle wall thicker? It must push blood to the whole body, not just next door to the lungs.
