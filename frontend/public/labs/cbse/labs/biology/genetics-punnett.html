Think about it

Cross two hybrids Pp × Pp: the classic 3 : 1 phenotype ratio appears. But look at the genotypes — it's really 1 PP : 2 Pp : 1 pp. Why can't you tell a PP plant from a Pp plant just by looking? That's Mendel's law of dominance in action.
