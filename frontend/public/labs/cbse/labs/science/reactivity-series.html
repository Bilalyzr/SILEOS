Observation

Pick a solution and a metal, then watch the beaker.

The rule

A metal can push out (displace) any metal below it in the series from a salt solution — because it's more eager to give away electrons. Copper in iron(II) sulphate? No reaction — copper sits below iron. Iron in copper sulphate? The strip turns copper-red!
