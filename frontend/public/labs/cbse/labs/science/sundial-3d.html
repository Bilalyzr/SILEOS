Think about it

The gnomon's shadow is the planet's own clock hand: Earth rotates 15° per hour, so the shadow sweeps 360° per day. Slide through a day — see sunrise-noon- sunset. Now slide the season: in summer the Sun rides higher (short shadow, long day); in winter it stays low (long shadow, short day). Ancient astronomers read this same dial!
