Think about it

Pressure: same weight, smaller area → deeper sink. That's why camel feet are wide and nails are pointed!
Friction: the surface tugs back up to a limit (static friction), then the block slides with constant kinetic friction. Ball bearings and oil reduce it; treaded tyres increase it.
