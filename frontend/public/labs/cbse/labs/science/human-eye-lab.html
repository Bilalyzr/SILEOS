Eye condition

Corrective lens

0.00 D
Add a lens to bend the rays onto the retina.

Think about it

Myopia (short-sight): eyeball too long — image forms before the retina. Fix with a concave (−) lens.
Hypermetropia (long-sight): eyeball too short — image would form behind the retina. Fix with a convex (+) lens.
Watch the rays: correct power = a single sharp point exactly on the retina 🎯
