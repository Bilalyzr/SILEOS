Think about it

Wind is just air squeezed from high pressure to low pressure. Day: land heats faster than sea → air over land rises → cool sea air rushes in (sea breeze, great for kites!). The Coriolis effect from Earth's spin bends winds — in a cyclone it bends them so hard they spiral into the low-pressure eye. Reverse it at night for the land breeze.
