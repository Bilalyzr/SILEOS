Right-hand thumb rule

Point your right thumb along the current. Your curled fingers show the field — circles around the wire! Reverse the current and every compass flips. Inside a solenoid the loops merge into a straight field: the coil behaves like a bar magnet with N and S ends (an electromagnet).
