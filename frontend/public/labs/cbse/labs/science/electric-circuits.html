Think about it

Watch the blue current carriers — their speed is the current! In series, one switch-off or a dead bulb kills the whole circuit, and bulbs share the voltage (dim). In parallel, each bulb gets the full voltage (bright) and works independently. Try switching bulbs off by changing the layout.
