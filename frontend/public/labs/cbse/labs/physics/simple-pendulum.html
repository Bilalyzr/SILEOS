Think about it

Notice what does not change the time period: the mass, and (for small swings) the release angle! Only length and g matter. Quadruple L — does T double? On the Moon everything swings lazily: that's how astronauts tested low gravity. At 45° the measured T runs slightly long: the small-angle approximation breaks down.
