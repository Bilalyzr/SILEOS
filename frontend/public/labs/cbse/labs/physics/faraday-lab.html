Faraday's law

EMF = −N · dΦ/dt. Only change induces current: hold the magnet still and the needle drops to zero, however strong the magnet. Move it fast → big deflection. More loops → bigger EMF. Reverse the motion → needle swings the other way (Lenz: induced current opposes the change). This is every power station generator on Earth.
