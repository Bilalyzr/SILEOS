How to play

Drag the orange dots of triangle A. B copies it with your scale and rotation. Same shape + same size = congruent (k = 1). Same shape, different size = similar (any k). Similar triangles keep equal angles and sides in a constant ratio — that's the base of heights-and-distances problems!
