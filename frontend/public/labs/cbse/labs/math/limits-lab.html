Think about it

Drag h → 0: the blue secant (through x₀ and x₀+h) swings into the red tangent. Its slope is the fraction f(x₀+h) − f(x₀) over h — a derivative is just a limit of slopes. Try h = 0.01: the numbers already agree to 2 decimals!
