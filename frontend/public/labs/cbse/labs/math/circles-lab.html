Think about it

Chord mode: the perpendicular from the centre bisects the chord — always, at any θ. Tangent: radius ⊥ tangent at the point of contact. Sector: area scales with θ/360 — a 90° sector is exactly a quarter pizza! Slice θ to 60° and read the numbers.
